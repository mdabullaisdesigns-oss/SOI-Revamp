@ signs-of-god
Q Why does the article say that believing in God is similar to believing in one’s own self?
* Each of us knows himself as a conscious being with will and power; God is such a Being on an infinitely greater scale :: Correct. “Man himself is ample evidence for himself.”
- Because man is part of God
- Because both beliefs are taught in childhood
- Because neither can be doubted by anyone
R “Since our experience of such a being is an everyday matter, what is astonishing about the existence of God, who also is a being wielding personal power, although on a scale far greater than ourselves?”

* Human ambitions exceed one earthly life; their fulfilment lies in the eternal world, for which one must prepare :: Well read. This world cannot hold all our dreams.
- Health advice is of little use
- People should plan to live longer
- Ambition is always harmful

* Treating people well even when they do not – patient, one-sided good conduct :: Exactly. It persuades where retaliation cannot.
- Skill in argument
- A large following
