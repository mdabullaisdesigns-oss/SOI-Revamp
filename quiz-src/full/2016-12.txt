@ the-american-election
Q Which instruction of the Quran does the editorial quote on how to meet hurtful words?
* “Ignore their hurtful talk” – keep to one’s own good conduct whatever others say :: Yes. This one-sided steadiness is called sabr, patience.
- “Answer them in the same way”
- “Leave their land”
- “Complain to those in power”
R “The Quran has commanded believers to ‘ignore their hurtful talk’ (33:48). That is, irrespective of the negative response the other person gives, people of faith should in every situation unilaterally behave positively.”
