Q How does the article reconcile “creation in six days” with the scientific view of a very long process?
* The Arabic word for “day” can mean a period of time, so the six days are six long stages :: Yes. Verses speak of a day like a thousand, or fifty thousand, years.
- By setting aside the scientific view
- By holding that the verses are not meant to be understood
- By taking each day as exactly 24 hours
