## 2017-05
@ post-elections-winners-losers
Q What does the editorial ask of political parties once an election is over?
* To stop treating each other as rivals and work together for the country’s good :: Yes. Like sportsmen after a match.
- To prepare at once for the next contest
- To challenge the result in every way
- To withdraw from public life if defeated
R “The party that has lost must accept its defeat without any sense of humiliation, while the winning party must take its victory without pride… All parties should focus on a single goal, that is, national development.”
@ the-form-and-spirit-of-ramazan
Q In form Ramazan is a month of fasting. What does the article say it is in spirit?
* A month of self-training and introspection in the light of the Quran :: Right. The Quran is like a mirror.
- A month of feasting at night
- A month of rest from all work
- A month of travel to holy places
R “In terms of form, the month of Ramazan is the month of fasting. But, in terms of spirit, the month of Ramazan is a month of self-training.”
@ the-form-and-spirit-of-ramazan
Q “God does not change the condition of a people’s lot, unless they change what is in their hearts” (13:11). What does the article say a sincere reader draws from this verse?
* That complaint against others is useless; reform must begin with oneself :: Yes. Then divine help follows.
- That nothing can ever change
- That change must be forced on others
- That only leaders need to change
R “He will realize that one has to completely abandon the language of complaint and protest against others… our own reform is the precondition enshrined in the Quran.”
@ sincerity
Q What makes a religious act lose its value, according to the article?
* Mixing in a personal motive, such as a wish for fame or to be thought noble :: Correct. Only God and the person know the intention.
- Performing it alone
- Performing it briefly
- Performing it without being asked
R “If one performs a religious act by adding some other, personal motive in it… the action becomes devoid of sincerity. It will have no value in God’s eyes.”
@ the-phenomenon-of-degeneration
Q A farmer whose land has turned barren begins by preparing the soil, not by harvesting. How does the article apply this to the renewal of a community?
* Begin with reviving the spirit and awakening minds, not with political power or outward forms :: Well read. Otherwise the cart is put before the horse.
- Begin by choosing new leaders
- Begin by building larger institutions
- Begin by isolating the community
R “If they start by reviving the spirit of religion, awakening the soul, and bringing about intellectual development, then it will be a right beginning.”
@ innumerable-bounties-of-fasting
Q What strength of character does the month of fasting aim to build?
* Fortitude and self-restraint – staying steady and positive under provocation and hardship :: Yes. Training for a whole life of patience.
- Physical toughness for hard labour
- Skill in debate
- Indifference to other people
R “In essence, it is training for a whole life of self-denial, inculcating patience, fortitude and forbearance.”
@ companionship-of-the-prophet
Q How, according to the article, can someone today experience the Prophet’s companionship?
* By sincerely following his way – for instance, treating an opponent as he would have done :: Right. It depends on inner affinity, not physical presence.
- By visiting his city once
- By collecting old manuscripts
- By dreaming of him
R “Companionship is not necessarily about being physically present with someone. It depends entirely on psychological compatibility.”
@ explaining-creation
Q The material world is orderly while the human world is full of problems. How does the article explain the difference?
* The universe was made to support man; man was given total freedom, and its misuse causes the problems :: Yes. Paradise is given on merit after this test.
- The Creator made the human world carelessly
- Problems are an illusion
- The material world is older and so wiser
R “It is this total freedom that creates all kinds of problems and not creation itself.”
@ good-parenting
Q Where does the article say the worth of parenting is really tested?
* Outside the home, in how the child meets life’s challenges :: Exactly. Self-confidence is the best gift.
- In the comfort of the home
- In the child’s school marks alone
- In how much the parents spend
R “It is not inside the home that parenting may be judged good or bad. It is outside the home that parenting must be judged.”
@ learning-about-life-from-the-family
Q The article calls the family a training school. What condition does it set for learning from it?
* To look at one’s own family members as objectively as one looks at others :: Well reasoned. Without idolising them or hiding their faults.
- To obey the eldest in everything
- To keep family matters secret
- To live apart from them
R “You must not idolize your family. You should view your family in just the same way as you view other people.”
