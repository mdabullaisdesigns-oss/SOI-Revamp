@ targeted-therapy
Q In medicine, targeted therapy acts on the specific genes that drive a disease. What does the editorial say is the “target” in reforming society?
* The thinking of the individual mind :: Yes. “As he thinketh in his heart, so is he.”
- The laws of the country
- The wealth of the community
- The leaders in power
R “The correct starting point is to address the individual mind with the aim of revolutionizing the thinking of the individual. This is the right way of social reform.”
@ signs-of-god
Q On seeing Maxwell’s equations, the physicist Boltzmann exclaimed, “Who was the God who wrote these signs?” What does the article draw from such reactions?
* The order and meaning found in nature point an open mind to a supreme intelligence :: Right. A biased mind stays in the dark amid the light.
- Mathematics is a form of worship
- Scientists are usually religious people
- Nature can be known only through equations
R “Every study of the Universe shows that it has not been randomly put together but impeccably planned… Nature is a sign of God and speaks to us about God.”
@ learn-the-art-of-ego-management
Q Does the article treat the ego as something bad in itself?
* No – it is a source of confidence and resolve; trouble arises when it is provoked in dealings with others :: Yes. So it has to be managed, not destroyed.
- Yes – it should be rooted out completely
- Yes – it is the mark of a weak person
- No – it should be given free rein
R “Ego is a healthy phenomenon… It is this negative aspect that creates problems” in social life, where differences lead to clashes.
@ learn-the-art-of-ego-management
Q The article calls ego management “the price of social living.” What does it mean?
* The benefits of living among others come only to one who accepts their “thorns” along with their “flowers” :: Well read. Each person is like a rose plant.
- Society charges a fee for membership
- One must pay others to keep the peace
- Living alone is the cheaper choice
R “If one wants to enjoy the benefits of social living one will have to learn the art of ego management… One has to accept the thorn in the same way that one accepts the flower.”

- Life is cold and hard
- Life melts away quickly
- Life is dangerous to those who come near
R “Only a small tip of his life lies in the present world; the rest of it lies in the world Hereafter.”
@ remembering-god
Q How does the article describe “much remembrance” of God (zikr-e-kathir)?
* A chain of reflection in which each thought about God’s work leads to the next :: Exactly. Rain, for example, sets a prepared mind thinking step by step.
- Reciting set words many times a day
- Remaining silent for long periods
- Attending many religious gatherings
R “Remembering God much, is a result of a chain reaction in the thinking process; that is, a series of thoughts in which each thought causes the next one.”
@ living-as-an-underdeveloped-personality
Q Why, says the article, do many people who were successful in their careers feel lost after retirement?
* For years they played a role and left their own personality undeveloped :: Right. Like an actor returning from the set.
- They no longer have enough money
- Their friends forget them
- They did not work hard enough
R “Having assumed the role of another personality in his professional life, he returns to his original self in his retired life. The original personality however has remained in an underdeveloped state.”
@ in-search-of-meaning
Q A lost child stops crying the moment his mother picks him up. What is this a picture of, in the article?
* Man’s search, which comes to rest only when he finds God for himself :: Yes. The test of having found Him is complete satisfaction.
- A child’s need for discipline
- The weakness of human beings
- The comfort that wealth brings
R “Everyone, knowingly or unknowingly, is in search of God… when you find Him, you should become completely satisfied, like the little boy.”
@ simple-living-high-thinking
Q Why does the article say simplicity can be called “a part of faith”?
* A simple life frees the mind from distraction, so that it can be given to higher things :: Well reasoned. “High thinking is the result of simple living.”
- Poverty is required of believers
- Simple people are easier to teach
- Complex things are forbidden
R “If we adopt simplicity in our lives, then we will save ourselves from many distractions. Only then will it be possible for us to use our faculties for higher thinking.”
