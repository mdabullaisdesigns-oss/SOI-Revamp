## 2019-08
@ islamic-society
Q What did the Prophet say about even a small gesture of kindness to an orphan?
* Even passing a hand over an orphan’s head with love and affection is an act of charity :: Yes. Orphans are owed special care.
- It counts only if money is given as well
- It is the duty of relatives alone
- It should be done only in private
R “Even passing a hand over the head of an orphan with love and affection is an act of charity.”
@ earthquake
Q After a severe earthquake, some called it a punishment on the people struck. What does the article say is the right response to such a calamity?
* To take it as a lesson and reminder for oneself, not as a verdict on others :: Right. Blaming the victims is called absurd.
- To work out which sins caused it
- To regard it as having no meaning
- To avoid the region in future
R “The correct attitude to natural calamities is that one should learn a lesson from them. Attributing them to the misdeeds of others will only bring down God’s wrath upon one’s own head.”
@ perseverance
Q A man noticed that waves had worn away a rock. What did he conclude?
* Even something as soft as water, acting persistently, can break something as hard as rock :: Well read. The sand of the shore is its work.
- Rock is weaker than it looks
- The sea is stronger in winter
- Nothing in nature lasts
R “Even if as soft a thing as water acts with perseverance, it can crush as hard an object as a rock into pieces.”
@ perseverance
Q The young astrophysicist Chandrasekhar had his paper ridiculed and rejected, and could find no post. How did his story end?
* He kept working, his theory was accepted, and decades later he received the Nobel Prize :: Yes. Rejection did not stop him.
- He gave up science for teaching
- He withdrew the theory as mistaken
- He was honoured at once on returning home
R “Ultimately his theory came to be accepted as Chandrasekhar Limit in the world of science. At the age of 73, in 1983, he was awarded the Nobel Prize for Physics.”
@ perseverance
Q Nature grows a cucumber in months but takes a hundred years over an oak. What does the article draw from this?
* Great success demands long-term effort; whoever will not pay that price must be content with small results :: Well reasoned. The same law applies to man.
- Fast-growing things are more useful
- Old trees should not be cut
- Patience matters only in farming
R “The indispensable price of great success is long-term endeavour. One who is not willing to pay this price should not expect any great success.”
@ paradise-a-known-entity
Q Why does the Quran call Paradise a “known provision” for believers?
* The spiritual joys they tasted in worship here are a foretaste of it, so it will not be strange to them :: Correct. “A fragrance of the garden” scented in this world.
- Because they were shown it in dreams
- Because its location is recorded
- Because others have described it to them
R “The sublime spiritual experiences one feels when God accepts some action are an introduction to the Heaven that God has promised… It will not be strange to them.”
@ nineteen-eighty-four
Q The world of nature is in perfect order while the human world is full of wrong. What resolution of this paradox does the article propose?
* Another world must come, in which God’s will prevails among men as it already does in nature :: Right. Fit for it are those who chose to obey while free to rebel.
- Human society will perfect itself in time
- Nature too will become disordered
- The paradox has no answer
R “The only way out of this paradox is to accept the coming of another world when reality will be enforced on man, as it is already enforced on the world of nature.”
@ two-methods
Q In the fable, the wind could not blow off a traveller’s blanket, but the sun’s gentle warmth made him remove it. What is the moral?
* Gentleness wins people over where harshness only makes them resist :: Yes. “God rewards gentleness and not harshness.”
- Strength always fails
- Slow workers are the best
- Warm weather improves the temper
R “By gentleness one can win peoples’ hearts. Harshness only drives people further away.”
@ growing-in-faith
Q A scientist said a day without a new discovery in nature was a day wasted. What does the article say of faith?
* Faith is the discovery of God and must keep growing; a faith that does not grow is hardly faith :: Exactly. God has no limit, so the discovery never ends.
- Faith should stay exactly as it began
- Faith belongs to childhood
- Faith needs no thought
R “Faith that does not grow is not really faith at all; it is just a manner of neglecting God.”
@ what-is-death
Q An aged political leader told Billy Graham that life had lost its meaning and asked, “Can you give me a ray of hope?” What does the article say provides that hope?
* The prophets’ message of an eternal, perfect world for those who prove worthy of it :: Well read. “God calls unto the home of peace.”
- A longer life through medicine
- The memory of past achievements
- The praise of future generations
R “It is this ray of hope that God’s prophets have come to the world to provide. The prophets have taught man that there is another world—one that is both eternal and ideal—after death.”
