## 2020-05
@ a-message-of-hope
Q Written amid the gloom of the pandemic, the editorial points to something seen every morning. What lesson does it take from the sunrise?
* The world moves from darkness towards light; one state gives way to a brighter one, so there is no cause for despair :: Yes. “Remove the word ‘hopelessness’ from your dictionary.”
- The day is short and must be hurried through
- Nature is indifferent to human trouble
- Only the early riser succeeds
R “There is a system in place in this world which brings light after darkness. Human beings are no exception to this principle.”

- “Man was created weak”
- “Every soul shall taste death”
- “The life of this world is but play”
R “The Creator gives us glad tidings of hope in the Quran: ‘Your Lord has not forsaken you, nor is He displeased with you.’ (93: 3)”
@ fake-news
Q What does the Quran (49:6) instruct when news is brought to you?
* Verify it fully before acting on it or passing it on, lest you harm others unknowingly :: Well read. And regret it afterwards.
- Share it quickly so others are warned
- Believe it if many are repeating it
- Ignore all news entirely
R “Believers, if an evil-doer brings you news, ascertain the correctness of the report fully, lest you unwittingly harm others, and then regret what you have done.”

@ intellectual-fog

* The trained ability to set aside the irrelevant and keep only what bears on the matter :: Well reasoned. The principle of elimination.
- Reading as much as possible
- Avoiding the internet altogether
- Following the most popular opinion

@ in-pursuit-of-truth
Q In the story, a hunter spends his life cutting steps up a mountain in search of the Bird of Truth and dies without reaching it. What comforts him at the end?
* A feather falls beside him, and he knows others will climb by the steps he cut :: Right. “They will find truth and through me.”
- He catches the bird at last
- He is carried down by friends
- He learns the bird never existed
R “Where I lie down, worn out, other men will stand young and fresh. By the steps that I have cut they will climb.”

@ seeker-of-truth
Q Which of these marks a true seeker, according to the article?
* He looks at the truth itself, not at the standing of the person who presents it :: Yes. Truth always comes through a human being.
- He accepts only what his elders taught
- He waits for the majority to decide
- He trusts only what is impressive
R “A true seeker looks not at the person who presents the truth but at the truth itself.”
@ acknowledgment-of-the-truth
Q Truth accords with human nature, yet people often refuse it. What does the article say holds them back?
* Psychological barriers – fear for their way of life or status, or prejudice against the one presenting it :: Exactly. A little honest thought would remove them.
- Lack of intelligence
- Lack of time
