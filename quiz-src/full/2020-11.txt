## 2020-11
@ the-question-of-intellect
Q How does the editorial describe the place of the intellect?
* It is a God-given faculty for reaching conclusions – an instrument, not itself a source of knowledge – and it supports revelation :: Yes. Reason and revelation are not rivals.
- It is the only source of all knowledge
- It is opposed to faith and must be set aside
- It is useful only for worldly matters
R “The status of intellect is as an instrument or faculty and not that of a judge by itself… intellect, or reason, reinforces revelation and does not contradict it.”
@ predicting-the-afterlife
Q The physicist Paul Dirac predicted antimatter years before it was observed. What point does the article make with his example?
* Science itself accepts unseen realities inferred from sound reasoning – so belief in the unseen Hereafter is not unreasonable :: Well reasoned. Inference came first, observation later.
- Scientists often guess correctly
- Theories should never be trusted
- Only what is seen can be believed
R “Even before these discoveries had reached the experimental stage, they were accepted as facts: they were not denied on the basis of the theoretical and abstract method of their presentation.”
@ predicting-the-afterlife
Q Which features of this world, according to the article, imply another world to complete it?
* It is finite, injustice persists in it, and human potential finds no full scope here :: Right. A perfect, infinite world must balance it.
- It is large, old and well mapped
- It is beautiful, varied and well supplied
- It is crowded, noisy and hurried
R “This world is finite: there must be an infinite world as well… Injustice prevails in this world: there must be a world of absolute justice.”
@ quest-for-another-habitable-planet
Q Commenting on plans to make Mars habitable, what does the article say such technology, if it existed, should be used for first?
* Restoring the Earth itself to a habitable state :: Yes. And man’s true future home is Paradise.
- Building cities on the moon
- Travelling to other stars
- Mining other planets
R “If it were possible for science to make a planet in space habitable for man through new technology, then the first application of this technology should be to reshape the Earth.”
@ the-eternal-journey
Q What does the article call the basic difference between a person of faith and one without?
* One lives as though he will stay here forever; the other knows he is on his way to the next world :: Correct. Hence a sense of answerability.
- One is cheerful; the other is gloomy
- One is learned; the other is not
- One is poor; the other is rich
R “One without faith lives on Earth as if he is going to stay here forever, while the hallmark of true faith is the belief that one is on the way to the next world.”
@ marifah-or-god-realization
Q “I don’t know, is half of knowledge.” How does the article apply this saying to the seeker of God?
* Admitting one’s ignorance awakens the spirit of enquiry; the more one admits it, the more one learns :: Well read. First a seeker, then a finder.
- One should hide what one does not know
- Half of knowledge is enough
- Knowledge is impossible to attain
R “First of all, an individual has to discover his own ignorance, and then a spirit of enquiry is born within him.”
@ secret-of-mental-peace
Q An astronaut who had walked on the moon said he still found no peace, nor later in business and wealth. What does the article conclude?
* Achievements cannot give inner peace; hearts find comfort in the remembrance of God :: Right. “Something was still missing.”
- He chose the wrong professions
- Peace comes only with retirement
- Great achievers are never content by nature
R “I thought going to the moon would give me peace… but they didn’t… ‘Surely in the remembrance of God, hearts can find comfort.’ (13: 28)”
@ secret-of-mental-peace
Q One cannot expect the comforts of home in a bus or train. What does the article mean by this comparison?
* This world is the journey, not the destination; full satisfaction belongs to the Hereafter :: Yes. The vehicle is only a means.
- Travel should be avoided
- Home is where one is born
- Comfort makes people lazy
R “The vehicle is only a means of your journey, not a substitute for your home. In the same way, the fulfillment of one’s desires is simply not possible for anyone in this world.”
@ trying-to-deceive-god
Q What behaviour does the article describe as “seeking to deceive God”?
* Dressing up self-interest in fine words, as though one were acting for the sake of truth :: Exactly. God judges by facts, not by what is said.
- Praying at the wrong times
- Forgetting a promise
- Keeping one’s good deeds secret
R “Their real purpose is self-interest. But they use words that conceal their true purpose, and show that they are doing so only for the sake of truth.”
@ the-word-of-god
Q “Let every soul look to what it lays up for the future” (59:18). How does the commentary divide human life?
* Into “today”, this world, and “tomorrow”, the Hereafter, where today’s deeds bear their result :: Well read. Success lies in keeping this in mind.
- Into youth and old age
- Into work and rest
- Into private and public life
R “The present world is a man’s ‘today’ and the world of the Hereafter is man’s ‘tomorrow’. Whatever a man does in the present world, he has to face the consequences in the ensuing longer life.”
