## 2020-03
@ paradise-lies-at-the-feet-of-the-mother
Q “Paradise lies at the feet of the mother.” To whom does the article say this saying is chiefly addressed, and why?
* To mothers – since children follow the path she walks, she should take care that it leads towards Paradise :: Yes. It stresses her role in a child’s upbringing.
- To fathers – to remind them of their authority
- To teachers – to honour their profession
- To rulers – to protect families
R “The Hadith about Paradise lying at the feet of a mother is addressed to the mother more than to the children. It emphasizes the importance of the role of a mother in the life of her children.”
@ quest-for-life-in-space
Q Scientists say a single message from space would prove intelligence elsewhere. What question does the article put in reply?
* If one message proves intelligence, does not the library of ordered information in a single cell point to an intelligent source? :: Well reasoned. The same standard should apply.
- Why has no message yet arrived?
- Who would be able to read such a message?
- How much would the search cost?
R “‘A single message from outer space would prove there was intelligent life on other planets,’ but an entire library of information inside the most simple cell doesn’t prove that intelligence put it there?”

@ end-of-the-age-of-war
Q What two features of the ancient world, according to the article, made war seem unavoidable to ambitious people?
* Hereditary monarchy and a land-based economy :: Right. Power and wealth could be gained only by seizing them.
- Poor roads and slow ships
- Illiteracy and superstition
- Small populations and harsh climates
R “The first of these was family-based monarchical rule. The second was the agriculture-based or land-based economy.”
@ end-of-the-age-of-war
Q What changes does the article say have removed those reasons for war?
* Democracy, which opens office through elections, and the Industrial Revolution, which opened countless ways to prosper :: Well reasoned. Goals can now be reached peacefully.
- Stronger weapons and larger armies
- Faster travel and better maps
- Fewer countries and fewer rulers
R “Political rulers now began being chosen through periodic elections… Because of this revolution, today innumerable new economic means and opportunities have become available.”
@ the-word-of-god
Q The Quran compares reliance on anything other than God to a spider’s web. Who, according to the commentary, is truly “learned”?
* One who can draw a great lesson about reality from such small signs in God’s world :: Exactly. Not merely one expert in books.
- One who has read the most books
- One who can win any argument
- One who has travelled widely
R “They are not those who have become expert in bookish discourses, but, rather, individuals who are capable of imbibing the sound advice conveyed through God’s signs.”
