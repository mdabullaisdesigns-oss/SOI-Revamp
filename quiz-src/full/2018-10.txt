## 2018-10
@ principles-of-islam
Q In a Hadith, God says on the Last Day: “I was ill, but you did not come to nurse Me.” What principle does the article draw from it?
* Whoever wishes to find God must serve those in need – there he would have found Him :: Yes. Worship of God and service to man go together.
- That God shares human weakness
- That only the sick deserve care
- That worship alone is enough
R “Such and such servant of Mine was ill. Had you gone there, you would have found Me there with him.”
@ principles-of-islam
Q How does the article describe the act of helping another person with what one has?
* As a way of thanking God for the blessings He has given :: Right. The helper has been given more, and spends it in God’s path.
- As a loan to be repaid later
- As a favour that earns influence
- As a duty only of the wealthy
R “On all such occasions when one man helps out another by virtue of those blessings which God has given him, he is in fact showing his gratitude to God for these favours.”
@ silence-and-god-realization
Q What does the article mean by observing silence?
* Not merely being quiet, but contemplating – which opens the door to God-realization :: Well read. “My silence should be silent contemplation.”
- Refusing to answer questions
- Living alone, away from people
- Speaking only on fixed days
R “Observing silence is not only to remain quiet but it means to contemplate… Silence is the door to Ma’rifat.”
@ unity-despite-diversity
Q What kind of unity did the Prophet’s Companions have, according to the article?
* Unity despite real differences – they were one on the central cause :: Yes. Unity does not require sameness.
- Unity because they never disagreed
- Unity imposed by strict rules
- Unity only within each family
R “Their unity was not of the sort that recognized no differences… in spite of all such personal differences, they were united on the central cause.”
@ unity-despite-diversity
Q What does the article call the bedrock of collective unity?
* Individual sacrifice – stepping aside, recognising others’ merit, accepting their opinion :: Correct. And a great common purpose ends petty disputes.
- A strong and feared leader
- Equal wealth for everyone
- Constant meetings and speeches
R “It means offering the best seat to another, standing aside while others forge ahead… Such individual sacrifice is the bedrock of collective unity.”
@ words-of-the-prophet
Q Asked what faith means, how did the Prophet reply?
* “Self-restraint and gentleness” :: Right. Faith shows in character.
- “Knowledge and argument”
- “Strength and courage”
- “Wealth and generosity”
R “When the Prophet Muhammad was asked by ‘Amr ibn ‘Abasah what was meant by faith, he replied, ‘Self-restraint and gentleness.’”
@ words-of-the-prophet
Q What does the Prophet teach about a trust placed in your care by someone who has himself let you down?
* Return it faithfully all the same – never betray a trust :: Yes. Another’s failing does not excuse one’s own.
- Keep it as compensation
- Return half of it
- Hand it to a third person
R “Never betray anyone’s trust, not even if the person concerned has failed to stand by his commitments to you.”
@ society
Q The Prophet stood up as a funeral passed, and was told it was that of a Jew. What did he answer?
* “Was he not a human being?” :: Exactly. Every person is worthy of respect.
- “I did not know”
- “Then let us sit”
- “Who were his family?”
R “The Prophet replied: ‘Was he not a human being?’ This tells us that every human being is worthy of respect.”
@ society
Q What did the Madinah Charter lay down for the different groups living in the city?
* Each group’s affairs would be decided according to its own religious tradition :: Well read. A model for a plural society.
- All groups would follow one common law
- Newcomers would have no rights
- Only the largest group would be consulted
R “Issues concerning the different groups domiciled in Madinah would be decided on the basis of their own religious traditions.”
@ success-through-superior-solutions
Q What do researchers say happens in the mind when a person faces a crisis?
* Latent abilities awaken and “brainstorming” produces a superior solution :: Well reasoned. Difficulties become ladders to success.
- The mind slows down to protect itself
- Old habits take over completely
- Memory improves but thinking stops
R “When a human being is facing a crisis situation, latent capabilities are aroused which enable him to engage in this process of brainstorming.”
