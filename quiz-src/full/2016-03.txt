@ islam-creator-of-the-modern-age
Q According to the article, what had kept people from investigating nature for so long?
* Nature’s objects were held sacred and worshipped, so no one thought to study them :: Right. The article says belief in one God removed this barrier.
- A lack of instruments
- The absence of writing
- Fear of long journeys
R “Holding the moon to be sacred was a major obstacle to even thinking of conquering it… Monotheism opened the doors of research and investigation by displacing nature from its sacred pedestal.”

@ peace-the-greatest-good
Q Why does the article name peace, not freedom, as the greatest good (summum bonum)?
* Peace never has a harmful result, whereas unchecked freedom can end in anarchy :: Well reasoned. Peace is like the soil in which all else grows.
- Freedom is of no value
- Peace is easier to achieve
- Freedom belongs only to rulers
R “Summum bonum refers to the maximum use of something which does not have any negative results, while the unchecked use of freedom can result in anarchy.”
