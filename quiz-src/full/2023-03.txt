* The sincere person, who meets difficulty with patience and positive planning, from the insincere, who reacts impatiently :: Yes. Hardship is life’s challenge-response test.
- The rich from the poor
- The learned from the unlearned

@ how-god-realization-impacts-ones-life
Q “God is great” is an obvious truth. What does the article say is the other half that must be grasped with it?
* That man himself is small – only then does the first truth produce humility :: Right. Every truth is a half-truth until one sees how it applies to oneself.
- That the universe is small
- That other people are small
- That greatness can be earned
R “To utter the words, ‘God is great’ is to acknowledge an outward reality, and to say, as a corollary, ‘I am small,’ indicates one’s willingness to mould oneself to this outer reality.”
@ a-life-of-purpose
Q Sailing alone round the world for charity, James Hatfield was badly burnt when his boat was damaged. Why did he say he forgot the pain?
* He was wholly occupied with steering his boat into port – his purpose outweighed the injury :: Well read. A great purpose makes one overlook harm.
- The burn was a slight one
- He had strong medicine on board
- Another sailor came to his aid
R “It was very painful, but I was so preoccupied with steering my boat into port that I forgot all about it.”

- A house full of books
- A house full of grain for the poor
- A house full of horses
