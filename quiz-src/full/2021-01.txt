## 2021-01
@ new-years-message
Q The editorial recalls a simple comparison about tension: “When it rains, you don’t demand that it stop raining…” How does it end?
* “…rather you use an umbrella” – find a way to manage stress instead of becoming anxious :: Yes. Tension is as natural as rain.
- “…rather you stay indoors until it ends”
- “…rather you complain to the authorities”
- “…rather you move to a drier place”
R “When it rains, you don’t demand that it stop raining, rather you use an umbrella. When faced with stress, find a solution to it instead of becoming anxious.”
@ creation-for-what
Q Modern comforts have made many old dreams real, yet joy still eludes people. What reason does the article give?
* They try to understand life from its short pre-death phase alone, leaving out the eternal phase after death :: Well reasoned. The whole gives the part its meaning.
- Comforts are still too few
- People work too little
- Happiness was easier in the past
R “There is only one reason for this tragic state of affairs. And that is, trying to understand life keeping only the pre-death period of life in mind.”
@ creation-for-what
Q With what image does the article describe the two phases of human life?
* This life is for sowing the seed; the life after death is for gathering the harvest :: Right. No fruit without sowing.
- This life is a road; the next is a bridge
- This life is a dream; the next is sleep
- This life is a lamp; the next is its shadow
R “The pre-death period of man’s life is the period for sowing seeds, as it were. The post-death period of man’s life is the period for harvesting the fruit.”
@ god-fearing-speech
Q How did Aisha describe the Prophet’s way of speaking?
* Slowly and clearly, pausing between sentences, so that one could have counted his words :: Yes. Never loud or coarse.
- Rapidly, to cover many subjects
- In verse, to delight his hearers
- Very rarely, and then at great length
R “The Prophet would not speak fast as you people do. He used to pause in between sentences, giving people time to memorize what he had said.”
@ god-fearing-speech
Q Why, according to the article, does a believer weigh his words before speaking?
* He knows every word is recorded and that he must answer to God for it :: Correct. Fear of God restrains idle talk.
- He is afraid of being laughed at
- He has little to say
- He wishes to appear wise
R “Aware of the fact that he will have to answer to God for everything he says, he assumes a deep sense of responsibility.”

- “Home is where the heart is; come when you wish”
- “A rolling stone gathers no moss”
- “The darkest hour is before the dawn”
R “Two men looked out from prison bars, one saw mud, and the other saw stars.”
@ right-thinking
Q She came to love the desert and wrote a successful book about it. What, according to the article, had actually changed?
* Only her own attitude – nothing in her surroundings :: Well read. A change within gave her a new life.
- The climate had become milder
- The villagers had learned her language
- Her husband had been posted elsewhere
R “Neither the desert, nor the natives, nor any other element in the situation had changed. Whatever change had taken place was inside the mind and heart of Thelma Thompson.”
@ training-in-resilience
Q Botanists say as much of a tree lies below ground as above. What lesson does the article draw?
* To grow outwardly one must first build strength inwardly – begin with oneself before rebuilding society :: Yes. “Root downward, fruit upward.”
- The hidden half is wasted
- Trees should be planted deeper
- What is seen matters most
R “If we seek to progress outwardly, we must first strengthen ourselves inwardly. We must begin from the base of our own selves before we can hope to build society anew.”
@ following-the-model-of-nature
Q What does a mountain stream do when stones block its path, and what does this teach?
* It flows around them – do not collide with obstacles, but find a way past and keep going :: Exactly. Avoidance, not confrontation.
- It stops until the stones are removed
- It wears the stones away by force
- It turns back to its source
R “The spring does not attempt to remove the stones to continue its journey. Instead, it carves its way ahead around and alongside the stones.”
@ following-the-model-of-nature
Q Sun, air and plants all give without taking in return. How is man’s following of this model meant to differ from nature’s?
* Nature follows it by compulsion; man is to adopt it consciously, of his own free will :: Well reasoned. Living as a giver by choice.
- Man may take twice what he gives
- Man need follow it only in youth
- Man is exempt from it
R “In the rest of the universe, the model has been established compulsively, under the laws of nature, while we have to adopt this model in our lives consciously, of our own free will.”
