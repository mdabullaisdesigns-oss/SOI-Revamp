## 2017-07
@ the-second-age-is-coming
Q The editorial calls the present earth “a nursery.” What does it mean?
* A place where people are tested and prepared, so that those of noble character may be chosen for the eternal world :: Yes. The true abode comes after.
- A place meant only for children
- A garden to be enjoyed without care
- A planet soon to be replaced by another in space
R “The present earth is like a nursery where human beings are meant to be tested. The person who gives proof of noble conduct and sublime character… will be chosen to settle in the eternal world of the Hereafter.”
@ the-role-of-difficulty
Q “It is not ease but effort, not facility but difficulty that makes man.” How does the article explain why?
* The mind releases more energy for a difficult task, and this builds a strong personality :: Right. A high target opens the reservoir wider.
- Difficult tasks earn more praise
- Easy tasks are usually forbidden
- Difficulty makes people fear failure
R “If we decide to do some difficult or great task, then the mind will release a greater amount of energy.”
@ peace-is-possible-but-only-through-the-right-way
Q “You cannot extinguish a fire by pouring fuel on it.” What is the wider lesson?
* Results come only by following the principles God has set, such as patience and positive action, not by emotional methods of our own :: Well read. The world does not accept excuses.
- Fires should be left to burn out
- Every problem needs outside help
- Strong feeling is the proof of sincerity
R “If God has made patience the secret of success in this world of His, you cannot attain success through impatience.”

Q The article says man’s freedom is “of action alone.” What has he not been given?

- The ability to think
- The right to choose
- The chance to try again

@ gods-wrath-and-his-mercy

- Because it needs no effort at all

@ lessons-from-nature
Q Animals use camouflage, staying out of sight and other means to avoid a fight. How does the article judge such avoidance?
* As necessary pragmatism taught by nature, not cowardice – it lets constructive work continue :: Well reasoned. Humans should do by choice what animals do by instinct.
- As weakness that humans should not copy
- As a trick suited only to small creatures
- As proof that animals feel no danger
R “They are not a form or expression of cowardice. Rather, they indicate a necessary pragmatism.”
