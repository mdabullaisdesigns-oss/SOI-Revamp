## 2014-09
@ avoid-confrontation-at-all-cost
Q What should one consider first before taking a step against another group, according to the editorial?
* The result it is likely to produce :: Yes. If the result cannot be achieved, patience and adjustment are the course.
- Whether one is in the right
- How many supporters one has
- What others will say
R “First of all, one should think about the result that this action would yield… if one is not in a position to attain the required result, then one must remain patient.”
@ avoid-confrontation-at-all-cost
Q The Prophet said: “Evil is lying asleep. The curse of God is on one who awakens it.” How does the editorial apply this?
* Do not let differences grow into confrontation, for the one who begins it bears the blame for what follows :: Right. Confrontation “does not know the language of justice.”
- Evil people should be left unpunished
- Evil does not exist until it is named
- One should sleep rather than argue
R “If we want to save ourselves from the ill effects of evil, then we must try our utmost to avoid awakening it. The beginner is the one who is responsible for the aftermath.”
@ adjustment-a-golden-principle
Q The article compares adjustment to keeping to one’s side of the road. What is the point?
* Adjustment is practical wisdom that keeps one’s own journey going; it is not submission :: Yes. “When you are not in the position to change others, change yourself.”
- Rules exist to be obeyed without thought
- Everyone should travel the same way
- The slower driver must always give way
R “Adjustment is not a submissive attitude; rather it is an attitude of wisdom… Adjustment gives you a chance to continue your journey.”
@ human-tragedy
Q “Men and nations do the sensible thing only after they have exhausted all other options.” What does the article say about accepting truth only when forced?
* Such acceptance has no value – and on the Day of Reckoning it will come too late :: Exactly. Worth lies in choosing rightly while free.
- It is as good as accepting it freely
- It is the only way people ever learn
- It shows strength of character
R “If one accepts the truth because one is forced to, one’s acceptance has no value.”
@ implementation-of-the-shariah-law
Q Aisha related that the first verses revealed were about God and the Hereafter, and commands came later. What method does the article draw from this?
* Reform begins with minds and hearts; rules follow once people are ready :: Well reasoned. Otherwise people would have refused outright.
- Laws must be enforced before beliefs are taught
- Belief makes laws unnecessary
- Commands should be revealed all at once
R “The enforcement of Shariah law begins in any society with intellectual purification of individuals, rather than with the practical enforcement of law.”
@ a-believer-lives-for-god
Q What change does the article describe in a person who begins to live for God?
* He prefers silence to talk, forgiveness to grudges, and has no wish for a privileged place :: Yes. He becomes unassuming.
- He withdraws from family and work
- He grows stern towards those who differ
- He seeks to lead others
R “He finds silence more gratifying than speech. He is happier to obey than to rebel, to forgive than to hold grudges… He is unassuming and self-effacing.”
@ willingness-to-change
Q The article calls the Prophet’s migration to Madinah “changing the place of activity.” What principle does it take from it?
* When work is blocked in one place, look for a more favourable setting; do not fight on to destruction :: Right. The move brought expansion, not escape.
- One should never leave one’s homeland
- Opposition must be met where it arises
- A mission should be paused in hard times
R “The desired course of action is not to remain at the same place, while pursuing an escalating and disastrous conflict… the desire should be to search out an alternative and conducive atmosphere.”
@ willingness-to-change
Q What does the article mean by “changing the field of activity,” seen in the Treaty of Hudaibiya?
* Moving the contest from the battlefield to the peaceful exchange of ideas :: Yes. Many then came to the Prophet’s message.
- Turning from trade to farming
- Moving the capital to another city
- Replacing old leaders with new ones
R “After this peace treaty, the field of confrontation between the two sides was replaced by the ideological field.”
@ failure-is-not-the-end
Q Why does the article say failure can be “greater than success”?
* Success breeds contentment, while failure stirs a drive that brings out hidden abilities :: Well read. Khorana, B. C. Roy and Bill Gates are the examples.
- Failure wins the sympathy of others
- Success is always a matter of luck
- Failure costs less effort
R “If you gain success, you become content. However, if you experience failure, then it generates great motivation in you.”
@ all-credit-goes-to-god-almighty
Q When success comes, to whom should the credit be given, and why does it matter?
* To God, who gave the person and every means he used; each success is a test of this recognition :: Correct. To credit anyone else is to fail the test.
- To oneself, for the effort made
- To one’s rivals, out of courtesy
- To good fortune
R “It is God Almighty who has created the successful person… If a person gives the credit to the Creator, then he passes his test.”
