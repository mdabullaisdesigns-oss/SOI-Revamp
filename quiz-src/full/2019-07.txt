Q When the throne of the Queen of Sheba was set before Solomon, what did he say?
* “This is by the grace of my Lord, to test whether I am grateful or ungrateful” :: Yes. Power is a test, not a prize.
- “This is the reward of my wisdom”
- “This proves my kingdom is the greatest”
- “This belongs to my people”
R “This is by the grace of my Lord, to test whether I am grateful or ungrateful.” (27: 40)
@ political-power-a-responsibility
Q The article speaks of two inner forces with which every person is born. What are they, in psychological terms?
* The ego and the conscience :: Right. Provoke the ego and it flares; the conscience corrects.
- Memory and imagination
- Hope and fear
- Instinct and habit
R “In psychological terms, they are the ego and the conscience—with which everyone is born.”
@ every-action-has-a-consequence
Q What advice did the Prophet give the man who promised to heed it?
* Before any action, consider its consequences – go ahead if they are good, refrain if bad :: Yes. Think before you act.
- Act quickly before the chance is lost
- Consult ten people before acting
- Do nothing that is new
R “When you decide on some action, think of its consequences. If they are good, go ahead with it: and if they are bad, refrain from it.”

@ complaints-take-you-away-from-god
Q A person wrongly deprived may receive far more from God than was taken. On what condition, says the article?
* That he stays free of complaint, remains hopeful of God, and wishes his oppressors well :: Correct. Positivity despite negative experience.
- That he demands his rights loudly
- That he repays the wrong in kind
- That he leaves his town for good
R “This eligibility is that in such conditions he remains, till the final limit, a non-complaining person.”
@ a-better-price
Q During a drought, merchants offered Usman high prices for his caravan of grain. What “better price” did he choose?
* God’s tenfold reward – he gave it all to the needy :: Yes. Faith in God’s promise made the sacrifice easy.
- A higher bid from Syria
- Payment in gold instead of silver
- A share in the merchants’ future trade
R “‘I can get ten dirhams for every one dirhams worth,’ he told them… Then he told them that he was giving all the goods to the needy people of Madinah.”
@ on-a-collision-course
Q A pilot saw another aircraft on a collision course and dived three hundred feet. What lesson does the article draw?
* Sometimes stepping back is wiser than pressing forward; avoidance saves both sides :: Well reasoned. Retreat can be the real success.
- Flying is too dangerous
- The lower course is always safer
- Instructions should not be trusted
R “Just as advancing is necessary to succeed in life, so is retreating… When there is fear of a head-on collision, avoidance is the only solution.”
@ the-thrill-of-discovery
Q Hans Bethe told his wife she was standing beside the only person who knew why the stars shine. What does the article say this joy should teach a believer?
* The discovery of God is far greater, and one who has truly made it will feel and share its thrill :: Right. No joy means the discovery is not yet made.
- Scientists are proud people
- Stars are less wonderful once explained
- Knowledge should be kept to oneself
R “Those who really believe in God feel the thrill of their discovery… If there are no traces of the joy of discovery, the discovery itself has yet to be made.”
@ the-test-of-man
Q Steel and atomic energy lay harmless in nature for ages. Why did they become destructive in human hands?
* Man is free, and weapons are a misuse of that freedom – freedom is a test, not a right :: Exactly. Self-discipline, not freedom alone, brings peace.
- Nature’s materials changed over time
- Scientists made mistakes in calculation
- There were too few resources to share
R “The reason is that nature has no freedom. Man, on the other hand, is free to act as he pleases… The freedom of choice that man has been given in the world today is a test; it is not his right.”
@ the-word-of-god
Q “Always measure with justice and do not give short measure.” How does the commentary connect this with the universe?
* The universe runs on balance and justice, and man is asked to weigh his words and deeds by the same standard :: Well read. The Quran states it; the cosmos shows it.
- The universe is too vast to be measured
- Traders alone are addressed
- Justice is possible only in the next world
R “The whole world surrounding the human being is based on these principles of balance and justice, which God wants human beings to follow.”
