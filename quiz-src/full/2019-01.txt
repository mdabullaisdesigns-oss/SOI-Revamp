@ from-denial-to-belief
Q Hoyle and Wickramasinghe each calculated the odds of life arising by chance. To what conclusion did the figures drive them?
* That life could not have begun accidentally; its conditions point to deliberate creation :: Well reasoned. Against their own earlier assumptions.
- That life is common throughout the universe
- That the question cannot be studied
- That earlier scientists had been right
R “Once we saw that the probability of life originating at random is so utterly miniscule as to make it absurd, it became sensible to think that the properties of physics on which life depends are in every respect deliberate.”

@ the-word-of-god
Q The verses speak of things “casting their shadows right and left, prostrating themselves before God.” What lesson does the commentary read in a shadow?
* Falling flat on the ground, it symbolises the humility with which man should bow before his Creator :: Well read. Everything around teaches obedience.
- Shadows show the time for prayer
- Shadows prove the sun moves
- Shadows are a sign of weakness
R “The shadow of a thing standing erect falls on the ground. In this way, it symbolizes kneeling down (sajdah). It shows symbolically how a man should bow down before his Creator.”
