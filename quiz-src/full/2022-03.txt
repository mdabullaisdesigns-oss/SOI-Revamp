## 2022-03
@ the-recipe-of-success
Q The editorial quotes a saying of the Prophet on how good qualities are gained. What does it say of knowledge and of serenity?
* Knowledge comes through learning, and serenity through exercising patience :: Yes. One who strives for good receives it.
- Knowledge comes by birth, and serenity by wealth
- Knowledge comes by luck, and serenity by solitude
- Knowledge comes with age, and serenity with sleep
R “A person gains knowledge through learning, even-mindedness; and serenity comes from exercising patience; one who strives to find good, receives good.”

* To acknowledge God of his own free choice, without seeing Him and without compulsion :: Right. The rest of creation acknowledges Him by compulsion.

- To use as much as he can

@ living-in-a-world-of-professions
Q What imbalance does the article find in people of our “world of professions”?
* They are highly developed in their professions but underdeveloped in the science of life :: Right. Able at work, at a loss in living.
- They know too little about their own trades
- They change professions too often
- They earn more than they need
R “While everyone is a developed personality in terms of their profession, in terms of their own nature, everyone is an underdeveloped personality.”
@ living-in-a-world-of-professions
Q What “simple formula” does the article recommend?
* Make intellectual development your priority, and manage the other parts of life around it :: Well reasoned. A question of priority, not just balance.
- Give equal hours to work and rest
- Earn first and think later
- Leave your profession altogether
R “Adopt a simple formula: make intellectual development your priority and then try to manage all other aspects of your life.”
@ book-review
Q The reviewer says we have two eyes for the visible world and a “third eye.” What is it, and what is it for?
* The intellect – by which we can understand the unseen world :: Yes. Reflection reaches where sight cannot.
- Memory – by which we recall the past
- Imagination – by which we invent stories
- Instinct – by which we sense danger
R “The third one is called ‘intellect’. It is the most important for us as we can understand the unseen world with the help of intellect.”
@ book-review
Q The cells of the body are wholly replaced over the years, yet the person remains the same. What does the review say this indicates?
* That there is something in us beyond the body that does not perish – the soul :: Exactly. So death need not be the end.
- That the body never really changes
- That memory is stored in the bones
- That people are different every decade
R “Our body cells are completely renewed and the old ones get destroyed. We nonetheless remain the same, this indicates that another portion is there which is immortal and that is soul.”
