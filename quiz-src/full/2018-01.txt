## 2018-01
@ entering-the-new-year
Q A reader learned from the magazine a new way of looking at anger. What was it?
* Anger releases great energy, and by staying silent in that moment it can be turned to positive use :: Yes. She learned to direct it into positive channels.
- Anger must be expressed at once to stay healthy
- Anger is a sign of strong character
- Anger can be cured only by medicine
R “If you remain silent in a moment of anger, nature will take its own course and anger will be converted into positive energy.”
@ every-moment-is-precious
Q “Live in this world as though you were a wayfarer.” How does this outlook help a person, according to the article?
* He bears losses without bitterness and keeps his true destination in mind :: Right. Even the urge for revenge is stilled.
- He avoids all responsibilities
- He never settles in one town
- He saves money for his travels
R “It will enable him to bear all kinds of afflictions and losses without embitterment, as at any moment he may be on the brink of a new life.”
@ every-moment-is-precious
Q “In the evening do not wait for the morning.” What does the saying urge?
* Use the present moment well; do not put off living rightly to a later time :: Well read. Each moment is too precious to waste on negativity.
- Stay awake through the night
- Finish all work before sunset
- Do not make any plans
R “Each moment of his life is precious—too precious to be squandered in negative thought or reaction.”
@ introspection
Q From what discovery, the article says, is an introspective mind born?
* The discovery of one’s own helplessness – that one has no power over life, death or what sustains life :: Yes. That is the secret of purification.
- The discovery of one’s hidden talents
- The discovery of other people’s faults
- The discovery of a wise teacher
R “An introspective mentality is born out of a discovery—that of one’s own helplessness.”
@ introspection
Q How, according to the article, is a person purified?
* Through his own struggle and repeated honest self-appraisal, led by conscience :: Correct. Not by some mysterious inspiration from another.
- Through the blessing of a saintly person
- Through living apart from society
- Through the passing of years
R “Purifying oneself involves struggle on the part of the individual. It is not at all related to any mysterious inspiration from some supposedly saintly person.”
@ the-importance-of-a-secondary-role
Q The article says a secondary role is not an inferior one. What special ability does the person who accepts it show?
* Ego management :: Right. For which the reward is double.
- Skill in giving orders
- Readiness to stay silent always
- A lack of ambition
R “Those who accept the secondary role will be rewarded doubly, because apart from their normal role, they have proved to have a great ability—ego management.”
@ worship-of-god
Q “Surely prayer restrains one from indecency and evil” (29:45). What test of true worship does the article draw from this?
* Whether it shows in a person’s conduct – modesty and peace towards others :: Well reasoned. Otherwise it is a spiritless ritual.
- Whether it is performed in a large gathering
- Whether it is long and frequent
- Whether others praise it
R “If one’s worship reflects in one’s behaviour, then it is worship in the true sense of the word.”
@ root-cause-of-terrorism-and-its-solution
Q What example does the article take from the Prophet’s peace treaty at Hudaybiyah?
* He accepted the other side’s terms to secure peace, and peace opened the way to constructive work :: Yes. “Ignore the problems, and avail of the opportunities.”
- He delayed the treaty until every right was granted
- He agreed to the treaty for a single year
- He left the matter to others to settle
R “In this treaty he unilaterally accepted all the conditions of his opponents… to enter into a 10-year no-war pact. This gave him and his Companions an opportunity to work uninterruptedly on a constructive programme.”
@ time-is-a-treasure
Q The article contrasts two ways of living. What are they?
* Merely passing time, and availing of time :: Exactly. “A day you have lived fully was your day.”
- Working and resting
- Earning and spending
- Speaking and listening
R “There are two ways of leading life. One is to simply pass time, and the other is to avail of time.”
@ reflections-on-life-and-death
Q In what sense does the article call the present world a “nursery”?
* People grow and are tested here, and those who prove fit are chosen to be settled in Paradise :: Well read. Freedom here is the test.
- It is a place for children only
- It is a garden meant for enjoyment
- It is where life ends for good
R “Human beings grow in the nursery of this present world, and in the world of the Hereafter plants from this nursery are selected for being settled in Paradise.”
