## 2017-02
@ positive-always
Q A young man was distressed by political news. What reminder did the author give him?
* History is in God’s hands, not any ruler’s; one should go on with one’s work and be friendly to all :: Yes. No political rule is permanent.
- He should leave the country at once
- He should join a protest movement
- He should stop following the news
R “It is God who shapes human history, and not any political ruler. Muslims must always adopt the attitude of: Do your work, and be friendly in your behaviour towards others.”
@ intellectual-development
Q The article says a believer lives between hope and fear, sometimes certain and sometimes doubtful about himself. Why does it see value in this?
* The unease keeps his thinking alive, so that he goes on learning and growing :: Right. Even the Companions felt it.
- It shows his faith is weak
- It keeps him from sinning by frightening him
- It makes him depend on scholars
R “The state of doubt is a sort of intellectual shock… one needs a mind-set that admits that one does not know, not a mind-set that insists that one knows.”
@ the-right-way-of-thinking
Q Animals act by instinct and are never “confused.” Why, according to the article, are human beings so often confused?
* Man is free and must manage his own thoughts, sorting the relevant from the irrelevant in a flood of information :: Yes. This is the art of right thinking.
- Human brains are weaker than animals’
- People have too little information
- Confusion is taught in schools
R “Human beings have to manage their thoughts by themselves… They need to have the intellectual ability in order to discern the difference between what is relevant and what is not.”
@ the-right-way-of-thinking
Q The article grants, for argument’s sake, that life developed by evolution. What does it say follows from that about belief in a Creator?
* Nothing against it – a theory about the process of creation says nothing against the Creator :: Well reasoned. This is given as an example of sorting ideas correctly.
- That there can be no Creator
- That creation happened in six ordinary days
- That science should be abandoned
R “The theory of evolution is about the process of creation, not about the Creator Himself.”
@ control-over-the-universe
Q What does the article say the faultless harmony of so vast a universe shows?
* That it is governed by a single all-powerful Controller :: Correct. Otherwise it would fall into chaos.
- That it is governed by many competing forces
- That it has no laws at all
- That it is smaller than it seems
R “This perfect harmony in the universe can be understood as a proof that it is functioning under an all-powerful God.”
@ my-home
Q People say “my house,” “my car,” “my property.” What does the article call these things?
* A “test-house,” a “test-car” – things lent for a time as a test :: Yes. At death they are all left behind.
- Rewards for hard work
- Burdens best given away
- Signs of God’s special favour
R “In this world, every single thing is for the purpose of a test… the things he had thought of as his own had actually been given to him by God for a temporary period.”
@ gods-guidance
Q The Prophet prayed, “O God, give me guidance and show me the right way.” What does the second request add, in the article’s explanation?
* Even on the straight path one needs to be kept from distractions that lead off into side lanes :: Right. Guidance is needed continuously.
- It repeats the first for emphasis
- It asks for worldly success
- It asks for a guide to travel with
R “Even after being guided to the straight path people have need of continuous guidance from God… ‘show me the right way’ means a request to be saved from distractions.”
@ right-and-wrong-thinking
Q What does the article mean by “selective thinking,” of which Satan’s refusal is the first example?
* Dwelling on the one thing one lacks while ignoring the many blessings one has :: Yes. It leaves a person without gratitude.
- Choosing one’s friends carefully
- Thinking only about useful subjects
- Remembering only happy events
R “They ignore the good things that they do have and make a big issue out of the things that they do not have.”
@ islam-peace-building
Q When a difficult situation arises, what course does the article recommend?
* Not to react at once, but to stop and consider the likely results of one’s response :: Exactly. Reaction sets off a chain of action and reaction.
- To answer immediately and firmly
- To leave the matter to others
- To act first and think afterwards
R “The right course is not to take immediate action but to stop and reflect patiently on the possible consequences of one’s response.”
@ unfulfilled-desire
Q A dying girl asked to be frozen in the hope of living again. What does the article say this longing for unending life points to?
* That man’s nature seeks eternity, which is to be found not on earth but in Paradise :: Well read. One must become a deserving candidate for it.
- That medicine will soon defeat death
- That the wish to live is a weakness
- That the young fear death more than the old
R “Eternal life is not possible on the planet earth. It requires a new world, that is, Paradise.”
