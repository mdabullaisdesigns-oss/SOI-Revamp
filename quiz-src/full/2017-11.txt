## 2017-11
@ putting-an-end-to-superstition
Q A solar eclipse coincided with the death of the Prophet’s infant son, and people began to link the two. What did the Prophet tell them?
* Eclipses are not caused by anyone’s death; they are signs of God, and a time for prayer and charity :: Yes. He corrected the belief though it seemed to honour his family.
- That the eclipse was indeed a sign of mourning
- That they should make noise to end it
- That such things should not be discussed
R “Eclipses of the sun and moon are not due to the death of any human being; they are just two signs given by God.”
@ putting-an-end-to-superstition
Q How, according to the article, did separating the Creator from the creation open the way to science?
* Nature was no longer held sacred and feared, so it could be explored and put to use :: Well reasoned. Awe gave way to enquiry.
- People lost interest in nature
- Scholars were paid to study the stars
- Old books of myths were translated
R “The awe surrounding nature was replaced with a spirit of research and enquiry… the phenomena of nature, looked upon by ancient man with reverence, have now become objects of investigation.”
@ deaths-lesson
Q For whom, the article says, is the careful ritual of burial really meant?
* The living – so that they see their own end in the dead and take a lesson :: Right. They “experience death before death arrives.”
- The dead person alone
- The family’s standing in society
- Those who dig the graves
R “Its significance is for those who are still alive. Through a dead person an important lesson is conveyed to living people—that they, too, will meet the very same fate one day.”
@ breaking-a-persons-heart
Q A petal and a feather are easily spoiled by a touch. What does the article say is more delicate still?
* The human heart – to wound it is a grave wrong in God’s eyes :: Yes. And onlookers who do nothing share the blame.
- A drop of dew
- A spider’s web
- A child’s toy
R “There is something even more fragile and delicate than a petal or a feather, and that is, the human heart.”
@ the-power-and-price-of-peace
Q Everything has a price. What does the article say is the price of peace?
* Willingness to tolerate loss :: Correct. Refusing to bear loss leads easily to violence.
- Great wealth
- A strong army
- Silence on every matter
R “What is the price of peace? It is to tolerate loss.”
@ the-power-and-price-of-peace
Q Why does the article say that patiently accepting a loss is not defeat?
* It keeps one balanced and able to rebuild with what remains – “after every evening a new morning dawns” :: Well read. It takes courage, not weakness.
- Because the loss is later repaid by others
- Because losses are never real
- Because no one else will come to know of it
R “To patiently accept and tolerate loss is not tantamount to defeat. Rather, it is something that requires great courage and boldness… one still has many resources left, on the basis of which one can rebuild one’s life.”
@ abstaining-from-baseness
Q A believer may err in a fit of emotion. What does the article say he does once the passion cools?
* Feels ashamed, seeks out the person he wronged, asks forgiveness and makes amends :: Yes. He errs, but does not stoop to baseness.
- Waits for the other person to forget
- Explains why he was justified
- Breaks off relations to avoid embarrassment
R “When the passion cools, one becomes conscious of the error and feels ashamed… The next step is to seek out the wronged person, beg forgiveness and make amends.”
@ realization-of-truth
Q The mind is compared to a mirror that reflects reality exactly. Why, then, do people fail to accept a truth placed before them?
* Something comes in between – an attachment, self-interest or pride :: Exactly. Like an object placed between a mirror and what it should reflect.
- The mind is too weak to grasp truth
- Truth is always hidden
- They have not studied enough
R “It is always personal attachments and commitments that prevent one from accepting truth.”
@ the-problem-of-stress
Q Why, in the article’s view, do holidays and outings fail to remove stress?
* Modern stress is intellectual in nature, and physical remedies cannot solve it :: Right. It needs a higher level of thinking – the remembrance of God.
- Holidays are too short
- Travel is tiring
- People take work with them
R “The real problem is that modern day stress is intellectual in nature, while people are trying physical means to de-stress themselves.”
@ the-word-of-god
Q The verses speak of the earth and heavens being formed in stages. What does the commentary infer from creation by stages?
* That it is planned – and a plan points to an all-knowing Planner :: Well reasoned. Order implies purpose.
- That creation was left unfinished
- That the universe made itself slowly
- That the earth is older than the heavens
R “Creation in this way—in other words by stages, means planned creation; and… it essentially follows that it has a Planner who has purposefully created it according to a set plan.”
