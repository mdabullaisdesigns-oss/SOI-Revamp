## 2019-05
@ ramazan
Q Ramazan is the month in which the Quran began to be revealed. How, according to the editorial, is this blessing marked?
* By fasting – an act of thanksgiving and acknowledgement – and by reading the Quran with understanding :: Yes. “O Lord I have heard and I accept.”
- By feasting through the day
- By public processions
- By exchanging costly gifts
R “The Quran is a reward from God to His servants. And fasting is acknowledgement of the reward.”
@ ramazan
Q How does fasting prepare a person for the study of the Quran?
* It reduces distractions and dependence on material things, turning attention inward :: Right. A prepared mind understands in greater depth.
- It leaves more hours free in the day
- It improves the memory through hunger
- It keeps one awake at night
R “The practice of fasting is also largely aimed at saving a person from distractions… Hence fasting prepares one for the study of the Quran.”
@ quran-and-hadith
Q The Quran says, “Say your prayers regularly,” but does not set out how. What does this example show about the Hadith?
* The Hadith supplies the details and the Prophet’s practical example – it is like a commentary on the Quran :: Well read. Text and commentary; theory and practice.
- The Hadith replaces the Quran
- The Hadith deals with other subjects
- The Hadith is part of the Quran
R “It is as if the Quran is the text and the Hadith its commentary; the Quran being theory and Hadith being the practice.”
@ the-angels
Q Which of these does the article list among the tasks of the angels?
* Carrying out God’s commands in the universe and keeping a record of human actions :: Yes. They also brought God’s word to the prophets.
- Deciding who will be rewarded
- Creating new forms of life
- Changing the laws of nature at will
R “It is they who carry out His commandments and communicate His word to the prophets. They preserve a record of man’s actions.”
@ honesty-and-hard-work
Q After Moses watered the flocks at Midian, one of the daughters urged her father to hire him. What reason did she give?
* “The best man to hire is someone strong and trustworthy” :: Correct. Hard work and honesty – a two-point formula for success.
- “He is a stranger and needs shelter”
- “He comes from a noble family”
- “He asked for very little”
R “One of the girls said, ‘Father, hire him! for the best man to hire is someone strong and trustworthy.’ (28: 26)”
@ the-gist-of-islam
Q Why, according to the article, does the Quran aim first at the individual’s thinking rather than at the system?
* Because the system depends on individuals, not the other way round :: Right. Change the person and the rest follows.
- Because systems cannot be changed
- Because individuals are easier to command
- Because society is unimportant
R “The actual target of the Book is to change the thinking of the individual… This is because the system is subservient to the individual and not vice versa.”
@ the-gist-of-islam
Q In social life, what decides whether we meet friendship or hostility from another person?
* Whether we have stirred his conscience or his ego :: Well reasoned. Touch the ego and expect reaction.
- Whether we are richer than he is
- Whether we speak the same language
- Whether we met him by day or night
R “If the ego is touched, we will face reaction from the other side. However, if we awaken a person’s conscience we will see antagonism turn to friendship.”
@ the-meaning-of-jihad
Q What does the article mean by jihad bin nafs?
* The inner struggle to control negative feelings such as arrogance, jealousy, greed and anger :: Yes. A lifelong effort to stay on the right path.
- A journey to distant lands
- A debate with opponents
- A period of fasting
R “Jihad is of two kinds. One is with the self (jihad bin nafs), that is making the maximum effort to keep control over negative feelings.”
@ clarification-of-a-misunderstanding
Q What did the Prophet pray on sighting the new moon of Ramazan?
* That the month might come “with peace and security” :: Exactly. Ramazan is the month of patience and self-control.
- That the month might pass quickly
- That the month might bring wealth
- That the month might bring victory over others
R “O God, let the moon of the month of Ramazan appear on us with peace and security.”
@ paradise-and-its-inhabitants
Q Which description fits those whom the article calls deserving of Paradise?
* People who restrain their ego, credit God for their achievements, and gladly take the back seat :: Well read. Living “with the blameless innocence of flowers.”
- People who always sit in the front row
- People who are known to everyone
- People who never met any difficulty
R “These are the worthy individuals who relish taking the back seats while others scramble for the front rows.”
