## 2017-09
@ hajj-should-inculcate-love-for-humanity
Q Hajj sets aside differences of race, colour and sect among the pilgrims. What does the editorial ask of the pilgrim on returning home?
* To extend that lesson of unity to all humanity and be a well-wisher of everyone :: Yes. He considers all mankind one community.
- To keep to the company of fellow pilgrims
- To perform the pilgrimage again each year
- To avoid dealings with people of other faiths
R “This lesson of Hajj, which is aimed at inculcating unity among Muslims, should be extended to entire humanity… He has to be a well-wisher to everyone.”
@ science-and-god-realization
Q Astronomers found the light of distant galaxies shifted towards the red end of the spectrum. What did this show?
* The galaxies are moving away from us – the universe is expanding :: Right. Like points on a balloon as it is blown up.
- The galaxies are cooling down
- The galaxies are drawing closer
- The galaxies are older than was thought
R “If all galaxies were being shifted to the red, it meant that they were all moving away from us. This meant that the universe was expanding.”
@ the-significance-of-hajj
Q The Prophet said the pilgrim returns as on the day his mother bore him. How does the article understand this?
* Hajj performed in its true spirit helps a person shed his conditioning and return to his pure nature :: Well read. A conscious process, not a miracle.
- The pilgrim forgets his past life
- The pilgrim need do no further good
- The pilgrim becomes physically younger
R “Hajj is a worship which facilitates one’s de-conditioning… and brings him back to his original nature.”
@ the-significance-of-hajj
Q After the Farewell Sermon, the Companions set out in whatever direction their horses were facing. What lesson does the article take from this?
* The end of Hajj marks the beginning of a new task – conveying God’s message to people :: Yes. “Another ‘Hajj’ begins on a much vaster scale.”
- Hajj should be followed by a long rest
- Travel is the purpose of pilgrimage
- The Companions wished to leave Arabia
R “This history of the Companions of the Prophet gives us the lesson that the end of Hajj marks the beginning of a new action.”
@ supporting-role
Q Why, according to the article, have people been given different capabilities?
* So that cooperation becomes necessary – some lead and others support in any undertaking :: Correct. Each plays the role suited to his gifts.
- So that the capable may look down on others
- So that people may live apart
- So that only a few may succeed
R “There is a difference in capabilities between men, so that mutual cooperation becomes a necessity in any undertaking.”
@ hell-and-heaven
Q “I have not seen anything as desirable as Paradise whose seeker is asleep.” What does the article give as the cause of this neglect?
* People concern themselves only with what they can see outwardly, so their minds are not awakened :: Right. The greatest issue goes unconsidered.
- People do not believe they can succeed
- People have been told too little
- People are too busy helping others
R “This neglect is because of people’s tendency to concern themselves only with what they can see externally. As a result of this, their minds are not awakened.”
@ motivation-is-greater-than-favour
Q A father rises by his own struggle, yet his children often do not match him. How does the article explain it?
* He began with discontent, which drives effort; they began with contentment, which kills motivation :: Well reasoned. Deprivation can be a privilege.
- The children lacked education
- The father kept his methods secret
- Success cannot occur twice in a family
R “The father started his life with the spirit of discontent, while his children started their lives with the spirit of contentment.”
@ the-comfort-zone
Q The article calls a comfort zone “a beautiful name for a non-creative zone.” What balanced advice does it give?
* Do not leap out of it from mere ambition, but when circumstances require change, accept it willingly :: Exactly. Change is “a circumstantial gift.”
- Leave your comfort zone as often as you can
- Never leave a place where you are settled
- Change only when others force you
R “It is not good, however, to jump out of your comfort zone simply out of ambition… But if circumstances require a change, do not be reluctant to accept it.”
@ the-next-world
Q “On the Day when the truth shall be laid bare…” (68:42). What does the article say will then count?
* Sincerity, truthfulness and effort made to please God – not show, wealth or fame :: Yes. Pretence cannot pass there.
- The number of one’s supporters
- The respect one enjoyed in the world
- Skill in speaking
R “On the Day of Judgment, only that person will be held deserving of rewards who had made effort in this world to please God.”
@ the-modern-science-of-theology
Q Why does the article contrast a “family prescription” with a scientifically prepared medicine?
* To show that the modern mind accepts only what is supported by evidence open to testing, so ideas must be presented that way :: Well read. Long usage alone no longer persuades.
- To show that old medicines were always harmful
- To warn against trusting doctors
- To praise family traditions
R “Today’s customer seeks that product which has been manufactured on scientific lines. Likewise it is only those ideas whose truth is verified by natural realities that he is ready to accept.”
