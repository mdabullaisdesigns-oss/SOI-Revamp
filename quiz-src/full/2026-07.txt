@ hidden-signs-of-life
Q Seeing a leafless tree covered in red flowers, what did Maulana Wahiduddin Khan pray?
* That God, who brings flowers from a barren tree, might bring spiritual growth into his own “barren being” :: Well read. Dryness is not always the final reality.
- That the tree might bear fruit
- That the journey might end soon
- That he might own such a tree
R “O God, I too am a barren existence. As You can bring such beautiful flowers out of a dry and barren tree, bring spiritual growth and realization into my barren being.”
