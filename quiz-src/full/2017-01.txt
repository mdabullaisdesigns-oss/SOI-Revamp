## 2017-01
@ discovering-the-modern-age
Q What does the editorial say has replaced political rule as the basis of influence in the modern age?
* Organisation and industrial infrastructure – peaceful economic strength :: Yes. Military occupation has become irrelevant.
- Larger armies
- Royal marriages
- Control of farmland
R “With the advent of mechanical power, political rule became irrelevant. Now, organization and infrastructure replaced political power.”
@ need-or-pandering-to-desires
Q What test does the article suggest before spending money on something?
* Stop and ask whether it meets a real need or only a desire :: Right. Spending on mere desire is wastefulness one must answer for.
- Ask whether one’s neighbours own it
- Ask whether the price will rise
- Ask whether it can be returned
R “Take time off to seriously think whether this is for something that you truly need or for something that you simply desire.”
@ god-a-source-of-conviction
Q Looking at the vast universe, what two discoveries does the article say a person makes?
* That there is an all-powerful Creator and Sustainer, and that man is utterly helpless without Him :: Yes. Daily life teaches the same helplessness.
- That the earth is ordinary, and that life is common
- That man is master of nature, and needs no help
- That the universe is empty, and has no order
R “Firstly, the existence of an All-Powerful God, who is its Creator as well as its Sustainer… the second discovery will be that of the utter helplessness of man.”
@ god-a-source-of-conviction
Q What does the article call a “psychological proof” of God’s existence?
* The feeling of dependence that every person meets in loss, sickness, age and death :: Well read. Our nature calls out that we need God.
- The comfort of belonging to a community
- The pleasure of success
- The fear of being alone at night
R “This feeling or realization is a psychological proof of God’s existence. Every person necessarily encounters this psychological experience.”
@ religion-a-divine-not-a-social-phenomenon
Q The article begins with nuclear energy, which can be misused for weapons. What point about religion does this lead to?
* A thing should be judged on its own merit, not by its misuse – Islam by its texts, not by what some Muslims do :: Correct. Theory must be separated from practice.
- Religion is as dangerous as nuclear energy
- Science and religion should be kept apart
- Religion changes with each society
R “The harmfulness or benefits of a concept or phenomenon relates to its user… If one wishes to understand Islam, one must look at it apart from the Muslims.”
@ respect-for-humanity-in-islam
Q According to the article, to whom is respect due?
* To every human being, of any religion or community, even where there are differences :: Yes. It is man’s birthright.
- To members of one’s own faith
- To those who first show respect
- To elders and rulers only
R “Everyone whether of one’s own religion or of any other religious tradition… is worthy of respect… human beings are to be respected despite their differences.”
@ respect-for-humanity-in-islam
Q Where does the article say our love and devotion for God find expression in this world?
* In our relations with other human beings :: Right. This is where faith is tested.
- In the size of our places of worship
- In how much we know
- In withdrawing from society

@ the-scientific-basis-of-rituals
Q How does the article describe the relation between ritual and the inner spirit of religion?
* Ritual is the outward expression of the spirit – like a shell to its kernel :: Yes. The kernel matters most, but the shell has its use.
- Ritual is the whole of religion
- Ritual is opposed to the spirit
- Ritual is a later addition of no value
R “Rituals are not just a set of rites; in fact, they are the external expression of the inner content of religion… like a nutshell without a kernel.”
@ the-scientific-basis-of-rituals
Q When a person performs a ritual while its spirit is, for the time, absent, what value does the article still see in it?
* It serves as a regular reminder that keeps awareness of religion alive :: Exactly. “A reminder of spirit through routine.”
- None whatever
- It earns the same reward as with the spirit
- It impresses other people
R “This minimum state is not totally worthless… It maintains the consciousness of religion and keeps one mindful of it.”
@ the-signs-of-hypocrisy
Q Which four traits did the Prophet name as marks of hypocrisy?
* Betraying a trust, speaking falsehood, breaking promises, and turning quarrelsome in a dispute :: Well read. To have even one is a flaw to be given up.
- Poverty, illness, old age and ignorance
- Silence, shyness, caution and thrift
- Pride of birth, wealth, learning and beauty
R “He betrays the trust of others; he speaks falsehood; he does not keep his promises; and when differences arise, he will not hesitate to fight.”
