Q How did the Prophet define a true Muslim, in the saying quoted in the editorial?
* One from whose hands and tongue people are safe :: Yes. A peaceful, harmless member of society.
- One who prays longer than others
- One who has travelled to many lands
- One who has memorised the most

@ the-existence-of-god
Q Science has fathomed only a small fraction of the universe. What does the article conclude about knowing the Creator?
* To demand complete knowledge of God is unreasonable; man finds Him through realising his own helplessness :: Well reasoned. We know “more and more about less and less.”
- God cannot be known in any way
- Science will soon explain everything
- Only scientists can speak about God
R “In this world a person can discover God only at the level of his helplessness and not at the level of absolute knowledge.”
@ the-reality-of-tazkiya
Q Tazkiya is usually rendered “purification.” What does the article say the word literally means, and what image does it use?
* Growth – as a seed in good conditions grows into a green tree :: Right. So it includes intellectual and spiritual development.
- Washing – as rain cleans the dust
- Burning – as fire refines gold
- Rest – as night follows day
R “Tazkiya literally means growth, one example of which can be seen in the tree.”
@ peace-for-the-sake-of-peace
Q “Peace is like rain.” What does the comparison mean?
* Rain only waters the field – the farmer must sow and work; peace only opens opportunities, which people must use :: Yes. Peace is a means, not the end product.
- Peace comes only in certain seasons
- Peace falls equally on all without effort
- Peace cannot be predicted
R “Peace is like rain. Peace only provides you with the opportunities. It is up to people to avail of these opportunities by wise planning.”
@ between-a-greater-and-lesser-evil
Q The article says that in life the choice is often not between right and wrong. Between what, then?
* A lesser evil and a greater evil – and the wise choose the lesser :: Correct. It prevents further loss and saves time for re-planning.
- Two equally good courses
- Speed and slowness
- Friends and strangers
R “In this life the choice is not always between right and wrong. The choice is rather between the lesser evil and the greater evil.”

Q “Others can create hurdles in your life, but…” How does the article complete the thought?
* No one has the power to decide your destiny; you can re-plan and reach your goal by another path :: Well read. A closed road means others are open.
- They can also remove them if asked
- They will tire of it in time
- You must create hurdles for them too

* God’s creation plan: this life is a test, followed by an eternal life :: Yes. “He created death and life so that He might test you.”
- The history of ancient nations

@ reality-of-life
Q Why does the article compare the Prophet’s warning about the Hereafter to a hurricane forecast?
* It concerns a coming reality we cannot alter; we can only prepare for it or be caught unprepared :: Well reasoned. It makes the matter personal and urgent.
- Because forecasts are often mistaken
- Because storms pass quickly
- Because it applies only to some regions
R “If the meteorological department forecasts a hurricane, it is telling us about an impending disaster in which those affected will have no say in the matter… One can either escape or expose oneself to destruction.”
@ the-word-of-god
Q Two men climbed into David’s chamber and put a case that was really an admonition to him. How did this powerful king respond?
* He understood, sought God’s forgiveness at once, and did not punish or scold them :: Exactly. He accepted correction, however it came.
- He had them arrested for trespass
- He refused to hear them that day
- He asked his ministers to decide
R “David enjoyed tremendous power at that time, but he did not punish or even scold the intruders… when his attention is drawn towards his shortcoming, he readily accepts it and rectifies it.”
