Q What does the editorial mean by “planning based on remnants”?
* Setting aside what has been lost and building wisely on what remains :: Yes. Germany, Japan and Singapore are the examples.
- Saving a little from every plan for later
- Copying the plans of earlier generations
- Planning only for the short term

* A rare arrangement made for man’s benefit, calling for gratitude :: Yes. Other worlds have nothing like it.
- An accident of astronomy

* When doors will not open, the fault is usually an outdated approach of our own, not enemies outside :: Well read. New locks need new keys.
- Locks today are badly made
- Hosts should be more careful
- Anger opens what patience cannot

* Following reason raises a person; being ruled by emotion brings him down :: Correct. Freedom of choice works both ways.
- Emotion raises a person; reason hardens him

@ false-pride

* By remembering the successes while forgetting the sacrifice that went before them :: Right. Success always follows great sacrifice.
- By reading too many books
- By studying other nations’ past
- By doubting what historians say

@ the-word-of-god
Q The verse 33:35 lists qualities side by side for “men and women.” What does the commentary conclude?
* The same virtues are asked of both alike, though they may show differently :: Well read. Ten qualities are named.
- Men are asked for more than women
- Women are asked for more than men
- The list applies only to the Prophet’s household
R “Just as these qualities are required of men, so also are they required of women. Although their manifestation may be different, the qualities are the same for both.”
