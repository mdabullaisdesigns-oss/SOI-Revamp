@ science-a-means-of-reinforcing-faith
Q Many expected the advance of science to weaken religion. What is the editorial’s view of what has actually happened?
* Modern knowledge has corroborated the truths of religion and strengthened faith in God :: Yes. “We shall show them Our signs in the universe and within themselves.”
- Science and religion no longer concern each other
- Religion has had to abandon its claims
- Science has stopped asking large questions
R “Far from having a damaging effect on religion, modern knowledge has served to corroborate and consolidate its truths.”

@ the-foundation-of-reality

- Gold was at last made from iron
- Iron was found to be worthless
- The old formulas were proved right

@ science-opens-new-horizons
Q A leader once mocked that a spacecraft had found no God in space. How does the article reply with a finding of modern science?
* Only a small part of the universe is observable to man, so direct observation cannot be demanded; God is known through His creation :: Well reasoned. Indirect knowledge is the only kind available.
- Spacecraft have since travelled further
- Space is too dark for cameras
- The claim was never really made
R “Only around four per cent of the vast universe is observable for man… man, given his present abilities, can arrive only at indirect knowledge about God.”

@ islam-and-science
Q We can calculate exactly how an arrow falls, yet not say why it returns to earth. What does the article conclude about the scope of science?
* Science describes how things happen; the meaning and purpose behind them come from revelation :: Well read. The two do not compete.
- Science will soon answer every “why”
- Such questions have no answers
- Calculation is of little use
R “Science does not negate the Creator, as its goal is to understand and explain the natural phenomena. It makes no claim to account for the underlying reason of the events.”
